WHY WE MAKE THIS

Our prototype was developed to address the challenges faced by campers at Mount Balakbak, Rodriguez, Rizal, particularly the lack of reliable electricity and emergency communication.

The lack of reliable electricity and emergency communication infrastructure at the campsite of Mount Balakbak, Rodriguez, Rizal significant challenges to both the quality and safety of the outdoor recreation experience of the campers. Campers are unable to recharge their devices, which are essential not only for their convenience but also for their navigation, documentation, and emergency contact. More critically, the absence of cellular coverage in the area means that in the event of accidents, injuries, or other emergencies, campers would have no reliable means of summoning assistance.

To address these challenges, this study proposes an Off-Grid Smart Charging Locker Powered by Solar Energy with LoRa-Based Emergency Assistance for mountain campsites at Mount Balakbak, Rodriguez, Rizal. The proposed system is designed to provide campers with a secure, solar-powered charging station for mobile devices and other essential electronics while integrating a LoRa-based emergency communication system capable of transmitting distress signals in areas without and unreliable cellular network coverage.

OUR PURPOSE

To provide campers with a secure and solar-powered charging station while providing an alternative emergency communication system for areas with unreliable or unavailable cellular coverage.
